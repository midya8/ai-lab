# Repairs

> Demo data: Harbour & Spoke Bicycle Co. is a fictional business created
> for this project's examples. It is not a real client.

## Standard tune-up

A standard tune-up costs $89 and includes brake and gear adjustment,
a drivetrain clean, a bolt check, and tire inflation. Parts are extra
and are always quoted before we fit them.

## Turnaround

Most repairs are ready within 3 business days of drop-off. A standard
tune-up booked in advance is usually ready the next day. We will call
you when your bike is ready to collect.

## Booking

Repairs can be booked online or in store. Walk-in repairs are welcome
when the bench has space, but booked jobs are worked on first. Loaner
bikes are available for $15 per day while your bike is in the shop.
