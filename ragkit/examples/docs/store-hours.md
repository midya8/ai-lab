# Store Hours

> Demo data: Harbour & Spoke Bicycle Co. is a fictional business created
> for this project's examples. It is not a real client.

## Regular hours

The shop is open Monday to Friday from 9am to 7pm. On Saturday we are
open from 9am to 5pm. The shop is closed on Sundays so the team can ride.

## Holidays

On statutory holidays the shop opens at 10am and closes at 4pm. We are
closed on Christmas Day and New Year's Day. Holiday hours are posted on
the front door at least one week in advance.

## Service desk

The service desk takes drop-offs until 30 minutes before closing. If
you arrive after that, we will book you in for the next open day.
