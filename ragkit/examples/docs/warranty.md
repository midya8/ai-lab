# Warranty

> Demo data: Harbour & Spoke Bicycle Co. is a fictional business created
> for this project's examples. It is not a real client.

## Frame warranty

Every bicycle frame we sell carries a lifetime warranty against defects
in materials and workmanship for the original owner. The lifetime warranty
covers the frame and fork only, and it does not transfer to a new owner.

## Components

Components such as shifters, derailleurs and brakes are covered for
2 years from the date of purchase. Wear items are not covered: brake
pads, chains, cassettes, tires and grips wear out with normal use.

## Making a claim

To make a warranty claim, email the shop with photos of the problem and
your proof of purchase. We will confirm within 3 business days whether
the claim is covered and what the next step is.
