# Returns

> Demo data: Harbour & Spoke Bicycle Co. is a fictional business created
> for this project's examples. It is not a real client.

## Return window

Returns are accepted within 30 days of purchase. The item must be unused
and in its original packaging. Bring your receipt or order confirmation
with you, since returns cannot be processed without proof of purchase.

## Refunds and exchanges

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days.
Exchanges are free and can be done in store on the same visit. There is
no restocking fee for unused items returned in their original packaging.

## What cannot be returned

Custom-built bikes, cut cables and hoses, and clearance items marked
final sale cannot be returned. If you are unsure whether an item
qualifies, ask a staff member before you leave the shop.
