# Shipping

> Demo data: Harbour & Spoke Bicycle Co. is a fictional business created
> for this project's examples. It is not a real client.

## Delivery times

Orders ship within 2 business days of being placed. Delivery within
Ontario usually takes 2 to 4 business days after dispatch. You will
receive a tracking email as soon as your order leaves the shop.

## Shipping costs

We offer free shipping on orders over $150. Orders under $150 ship for
a flat rate of $12 anywhere in Ontario. Shipping outside Ontario is
calculated at checkout.

## Restrictions

We cannot ship bicycles to PO boxes, because a signature is required
on delivery. Accessories and parts can be shipped to a PO box. If your
delivery address is a PO box, choose in-store pickup instead.
