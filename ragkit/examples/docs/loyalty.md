# Loyalty Program

> Demo data: Harbour & Spoke Bicycle Co. is a fictional business created
> for this project's examples. It is not a real client.

## Spoke Club

Spoke Club members earn 1 point for every $1 spent in store or online.
When you reach 200 points you earn a $10 reward, which is applied
automatically at your next purchase. Points expire after 12 months.

## Birthday bonus

Members receive a birthday bonus of 50 points in their birthday month.
The bonus is added automatically and does not require a purchase.

## Joining

Joining Spoke Club is free and takes less than a minute at the till or
online. You can check your points balance at any time by asking in
store or signing in to your account.
